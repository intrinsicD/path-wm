# Problem

Establish a learning, modular published world-model baseline with explicit multiple-dataset protocols before testing research extensions.

## Current integrated objective — 22 September 2026

Provenance: user. Decision: N532. The earlier baseline objective above supplies
components for the integrated research goal rather than its complete capability
proof. Demonstrate compatible latent perception, thinking, action and memory,
preferring native interfaces while allowing adapters; shared-depth loops; meaningful
concepts and instances; autonomous knowledge-graph acquisition, retrieval and
correction; and reusable workflows across domains. Runtime knowledge acquisition
and correction should not require explicit model-weight retraining. Latent diffusion
is a tentative mechanism (N533/O374), not a required or validated result.

Scope and proposed evidence: [integrated goal](../../docs/integrated-latent-agent-goal.md).
The objective is adopted; demonstration, training recipe and performance remain open.
